    Biomes

    The forge re-rolls its biome every 40 s. Rarer biomes grant more Resonance.

    Resonance is how biomes make you lucky. At Resonance N the forge draws the rarity table N times and keeps the best result — so P(tier or better) becomes 1 − (1 − p)N. The weight table itself is never touched, which is why the odds in the Codex stay exact.
